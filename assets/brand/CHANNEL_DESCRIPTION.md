Every famous ship has an ending. Most people never hear it.

Fleet's End tells the true stories of what happened to the ships after the headlines: aircraft carriers sunk on purpose, battleships blown up by atomic bombs, warships sold for a penny, ghost fleets left to rot, and wrecks found miles below the surface.

Each documentary follows one ship to its final chapter, using real archive film and photographs from the US Navy and the National Archives, maps that show exactly where it happened, and the official records behind every claim.

New ship stories every week. Subscribe and find out what happened to the ships you thought you knew.

Narration on this channel is AI-generated. Research, scripts and facts are checked against official sources. Spot an error? Tell us in the comments and we'll correct it.
