# FACT-CHECK A: build, service, fire, shoot-downs, limbo (USS Oriskany CV-34)

Checker A of 3 (CLAUDE.md gate). Scope: build, commissioning, SCB-27A / SCB-125A, length, Korea, Vietnam,
26 Oct 1966 fire, Stockdale and McCain shoot-downs, 1976 decommissioning, failed museum and scrap sales
(1990s). Everything below was re-checked against the sources listed, not copied from the dossier.
Checked 4 Nov 2026.

Verdicts: **CONFIRMED** = the dossier is right and an official or primary source backs it ·
**CORRECTED** = the core is right but a detail, figure or wording must change · **WRONG** = the dossier claim
is false · **UNVERIFIED** = official sources disagree or nothing official was found; don't narrate as fact.

## Safe narration wording (disputed items)

1. **Fire toll (44 vs 43):** "The fire killed forty-four men." If the script wants the nuance: "Forty-three died that
   morning. A forty-fourth died of his burns days later." Don't say "43" alone, and don't say "44 died
   instantly". (Five were killed in the first blast; DANFS.)
2. **Fire injuries:** "Dozens more were hurt." No number (38 vs 156).
3. **Fire cause:** "A sailor, in a panic, threw a burning flare into a locker packed with about seven hundred
   more." Don't name him or blame him: a court-martial acquittal is reported (Wikipedia, unverified).
4. **Fire duration:** "It burned for about seven hours." No "3 hours".
5. **McCain location:** "Shot down over Hanoi; he came down in a lake in the middle of the city." Never "south of
   Hanoi". Never "VA-46 off Oriskany" (he was VA-163).
6. **McCain release:** "March 1973" or "14 March 1973".
7. **Stockdale MoH:** "In 1976 he received the Medal of Honor." (Exact day differs: 4 vs 6 March.)
8. **Length:** "nearly 900 feet". Don't say "911 feet as rebuilt" (no source makes that link). "1,000 ft" is wrong.
9. **Battle stars:** don't give a Vietnam number (DANFS 10 vs FWC/Navy 5).
10. **First scrap sale:** "In the early 1990s" (date disputed: Jan 1993 vs 1994).
11. **Pegasus:** "In September 1995 a start-up called Pegasus bought her for scrap for just over a million
    dollars" (GAO: high bid >$1.2M). "In 1997 the Navy took her back." No exact termination day.
12. **The scrap saga in one line:** "Two scrap deals collapsed and two more found no buyer." Avoid DANFS's
    "three contracts" because its order of events is wrong.
13. **Williams dogfight:** "officially credited with one at the time; in 2023 the Navy cited him for four."
14. **1953 deck explosion:** "killed two and wounded more than a dozen".
