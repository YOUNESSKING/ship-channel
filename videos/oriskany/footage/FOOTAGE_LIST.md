# USS Oriskany (CV-34) - footage list

Built 2026-11-04 for the ~18-min Oriskany video. Every NARA item below was opened through the catalog API and its `useRestriction` field read; NHHC and Wikimedia pages were read through a fetch service (this machine cannot download from either: NHHC fails TLS, Wikimedia returns HTTP 429). Files sit in `src/` (git-ignored): `src/photos/` (NARA/LoC stills), `src/film/` (full NARA reels), `src/clips/` (6 s cuts made by `cut_clips.sh`, 1920x1080, 30 fps, no audio, 4:3 pillarboxed; `MODE=fill` crops to fill instead).

**Rights basis.** NARA RG 428 (US Navy), RG 19 (Bureau of Ships), RG 330 (DoD/DVIC) and RG 80 items are works of the US federal government = public domain. NARA lists most RG 428 film as useRestriction "Undetermined" (its default for un-reviewed reels); they are Navy-produced and federal. 208-UN-177 is a United News (Office of War Information) newsreel, NARA "Unrestricted", also on archive.org as CC0 (`gov.archives.arc.39085`). HAER (LoC) = "No known restrictions on images made by the U.S. Government". Wikimedia items: licence quoted per file. Never NC/ND.

**Honesty rule.** The "does NOT show" column is binding: never caption a shot with a place, date or person it does not verifiably show.
