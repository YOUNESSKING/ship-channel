# Titles and thumbnails: USS Oriskany

Built from `research/FORGOTTEN_FLEET_STUDY.md` (sections 3a–3c and the final DO/DON'T) and CLAUDE.md packaging
rules. Every claim below is true and traced to the fact-checks.

Packaging lessons applied:
- The topic gate comes first. The ship is famous, no one has covered the answer, and the answer surprises (study DO 1–2).
- No "Where Is X Now" template: without a film or star link that template's median is about 3K (DON'T 1). No years,
  no brackets, no emojis. Aim for under ~50 characters, as 68 % of FF's titles are.
- No explainer framing ("How the Navy...", "The History of..."): FF's explainers all flopped (DON'T 11).
- Don't clone FF's template (yellow condensed text, AI photoreal ship). Use real 2006 Navy photos of the actual ship
  only, with no AI ships and no tool watermarks (DO 12, DON'T 4 and 7).
- Thumbnail text is 1–3 true words. Check every thumbnail at sidebar size (168×94) and on a feed-size strip.
- vidIQ title scoring (≥ 85 each) and Test & Compare are **still to do**. They cost credits (spend gate: ask the
  owner first, one vidIQ job at a time, keep ≥ 100 credits).
- Publish by 11 Nov 2026. No title or thumbnail mentions the SS United States date, so nothing goes stale on the 13th.
