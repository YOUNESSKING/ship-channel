**MED** = one official source, or reliable sources that differ in detail · **UNVERIFIED** = fewer than two reliable
sources, or only press/secondary/estimate. **Nothing here has passed the 3-checker gate yet (CLAUDE.md).**
Narrative and reasoning: `RESEARCH.md`. Compiled 2026-11-17.

## Conflicts summary (script must pick one wording)
1. **Bomb hits at Pearl Harbor:** "at least 6, possibly 10" (AR41) / 2–3 before sortie + more (DANFS63) / 5 in the second wave (BuShips via PHAM).
2. **Pearl Harbor dead:** 50 KIA (NHHC-PH1, PW) vs 60 killed + 109 wounded (DANFS63); wounded 109 vs 105.
3. **Torpedoes at Pearl Harbor:** 1 (AR41, NHHC) vs "one or possibly two" (PW).
4. **Where she grounded:** Hospital Point ~0910 (temporary) then Waipio Point 1045 (deliberate, stern-first). Popular accounts give only Hospital Point.
5. **Able miss distance:** 1,500–2,000 ft (BAB47) / 710 yd (NPS37) / 2,130 ft (JMA22) / ~1,750 ft (IOM96) / ~2,110 ft (NWA); ship-to-burst 600 / 615 / 639–811 yd.
6. **Able yield:** 23 kt (DNA, IOM) vs ~20 kt (NPS37, JMA22). Burst height 518 vs 520 ft.
7. **Gilliam distance from burst:** 50 yd (NPS37) vs 150 yd (BUAER).
8. **Baker distance:** 1,060 yd (BUAER) vs 1,000 yd (JMA22).
9. **Target fleet:** 95 identified / 88 per shot / 90 anchored at Able / 84 tracked / 71 (NWA).
10. **Personnel:** 42,000 (BAB47, IOM96) vs 39,418 Navy only (DNA82-1).
11. **Decommissioning place:** Kwajalein (DNA82-2) vs Pearl Harbor (DANFS63). Date 29 Aug 1946 agreed.
12. **Ships that sank her:** Iowa, Astoria, Pasadena (logs, JMA22) vs Springfield also (USNI 1948 announcement).
13. **Final blow:** "aerial torpedoes" plural / "five aerial torpedo strikes" (press) vs popular "one torpedo amidships".
14. **Sinking position:** "65 miles SW" vs DNA82-2 coordinates that compute to ~76 nmi WSW; wreck found 65 nmi SW (SEARCH).
15. **Oklahoma guns in Nevada:** one (JMA22) vs two (NHHC-COMM snippet).
16. **"Only ship at both Pearl Harbor and Normandy" / "D-Day flagship"** (NPS-BSR) vs "only battleship" (derived). Use "only battleship".
17. **"Only battleship to survive two atomic bombs":** FALSE (New York, Pennsylvania also).
