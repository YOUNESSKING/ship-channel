## NHHC (history.navy.mil): search here by hand
- Nevada (BB-36) photo pages: salvage 1942 (refloating, drydock, Pearl Harbor Navy Yard), Puget Sound rebuild 1942-43, Attu 1943, Utah Beach and Cherbourg June 1944, Toulon Aug 1944, Iwo Jima and Okinawa 1945, Crossroads (orange paint, post-Able, post-Baker), Pearl Harbor May 1947 radiological tests, and "Nevada 1948" (any NH-series print of the sinking). The Nevada page is `history.navy.mil/our-collections/photography/us-navy-ships/battleships/nevada-bb-36.html`.
- Portraits: Chief Boatswain Edwin J. Hill; Machinist Donald K. Ross; Lieutenant Commander Francis J. Thomas (look for 80-G or NH numbers, "Medal of Honor" pages; also Commons categories for each name).
- Admirals/others named in the script: Rear Admiral W. H. P. Blandy (Crossroads); the Navy officer who said "She was a grand old ship".

## Do not use
- Commons `File:Nevada during Operation Crossroads 1946.jpg` and `File:USS Nevada during Operation Crossroads 1946.jpg`: tagged "CC BY-SA 4.0 / Own work / Jdogmad, 2021": implausible for a 1946 photo (probably colourised or re-posted). 
- Commons `File:USS Nevada (BB-36) after ABLE.jpg`: an Arthur Beaumont painting; copyright unclear.
- Any newsreel (Universal, Movietone, Paramount, Pathe), TV documentary, "de-oldify" colourised upload (CC BY-NC-ND), PeriscopeFilm item, or the finished John Ford "December 7th" film for real-event shots. NARA 428-NPC-20012 is a Paramount News reel: not used.
- Ocean Infinity / SEARCH, Inc. imagery of the wreck (2020): no free licence found; written permission needed.
