In December 2014 the US Navy let go of the aircraft carrier USS Ranger for one cent. She had flown combat missions over Vietnam and in the Gulf War, and she had been on the big screen in Top Gun and Star Trek IV. The headlines said she was sold for a penny. But the strangest part of the deal was who paid whom. How does a 1,000-foot warship end up worth less than nothing, and what is left of her today?
