# FACT-CHECK C: USS Ranger (CV-61), the tow (Mar-Jul 2015) and the scrapping (2015-2017)

Checker C (independent). Checked 19 Nov 2026 against pages opened in this session: DVIDS photo 1799349 (Navy caption),
Peninsula Daily News (PDN) 6 Mar and 9 Mar 2015, The Columbian (AP) 6 Mar 2015, AP via Military Times 13 Jul 2015,
Foss Tow Bitts April 2015 (PDF text extracted), Port Isabel-South Padre Press 10 Jul 2015, American Recycler and MarineLink
(EMR completion release), Maritime Executive on Independence (Apr 2017), UPI 1 Nov 1983. Wikipedia not used.
Not reachable: navsea.navy.mil (403, release and EA), history.navy.mil, web.archive.org, warhistoryonline (404).
Not found at all: Brownsville Herald's own story, any Lexington Museum statement, any Ranger bell/anchor/prop location.

## Headline findings
1. **Departure CONFIRMED: Thursday 5 Mar 2015, about 9 a.m., Bremerton.** DVIDS (official) dated 5 Mar 2015 (Navy photo
   by MC3 Seth Coulter); AP "left Bremerton on March 5"; PDN day-2 report: "left Bremerton around 9 a.m. Thursday".
   Script-safe: "On March 5, 2015, tugs pulled her out of Bremerton."
2. **Tugs CORRECTED: the ocean tow was Crosby Tugs (Golden Meadow, Louisiana), lead tug "Crosby Leader".** Foss Tow Bitts
   (Apr 2015): Lindsey Foss led four Foss tugs (Lindsey, Wedell, Henry Foss, Pacific Knight) out of the berth, then
   "handed the ship off to an ocean towing vessel operated by Crosby Tugs". PDN: "Crosby Leader, the tug which is pulling
   the Ranger (there are also escort tugs)". The sheet's "Lindsey Foss" is the harbour tug only. Safe: "Harbour tugs
   eased her out; a Louisiana ocean tug, the Crosby Leader, took the long tow." Crosby Leader for the whole voyage rests on
   one PDN day-2 article: say "was towed by Crosby Tugs", not that the same boat did every mile.
3. **Route UNVERIFIED for the actual track.** PDN 9 Mar: "via the tip of Cape Horn". AP/Columbian/PDN 6 Mar: "around
   South America". No Ranger-specific source found for the Strait of Magellan (Independence 2017 did use it, Maritime
   Executive). Safe: "around the southern tip of South America".
4. **Distance: "about 16,000 miles" in AP/Columbian and PDN; Port Isabel-South Padre Press says 17,000.** Unit never
   stated. Safe: "roughly 16,000 miles".
5. **Panama: CONFIRMED as press statement only.** PDN: "too big to fit through the Panama Canal"; Columbian same. No
   official lock dimension or Ranger beam found. Safe: "too big for the Panama Canal". Do not give foot figures.
6. **Arrival CONFIRMED: Sunday 12 Jul 2015, Port of Brownsville** (AP caption "Sunday, July 12, 2015"; crowds at South
   Padre jetties; Port Isabel-South Padre Press expected 7-7:30 a.m.; pilots said about 6 h at 4-5 knots under tow in the
   last stretch). **129 days** is our arithmetic (5 Mar to 12 Jul), no source states it; AP gives none. The tow was
   forecast "five months" (Columbian). Safe: "a little over four months" / "129 days".
7. **Scrapping done 1 Nov 2017 CONFIRMED (EMR release, two reposts; one source family).** And **34 years to the day**
   CONFIRMED: UPI dated Tue 1 Nov 1983, six killed, 35+ injured, fire 9:50 a.m. local (northern Arabian Sea); EMR release
   itself says "34 years after" the MMR4 fire. EMR's 9:50 a.m. moment of silence matching the fire's 9:50 a.m. is in
   the sheet from EMR; the 1983 time is in UPI (both read), so the echo is supported. "Just over two years" of
   dismantling is EMR's phrase.
8. **NEW: tonnage. "Over 56,000 tons" recovered and recycled, most metal sold to domestic mills** (American Recycler and
   MarineLink, both read). Missing from the sheet. Source is EMR's own release; say "the yard says over 56,000 tons".
9. **"Over five tons of historic items" went to the USS Lexington Museum, Corpus Christi: stated by EMR only.** I found
   no Lexington-side confirmation and no list of what the five tons were. Script-safe: "the shipbreaker says more than
   five tons of historic items went to the Lexington museum". Do not name a single object there.
10. **MMR4 control-room items (gauges, windows, controls) to the USS Ranger Association for an exhibit to the six dead:
    EMR release and Maritime Executive.** Association asked Jan 2016 (Maritime Executive). Where the exhibit is: unknown.
11. **Bell, anchor, props, plaques: still UNVERIFIED, no source.** Say nothing about them. Souvenirs: a commemorative dog
    tag from her brass and copper, and engraved flight-deck sections for sale (EMR/MarineLink).
