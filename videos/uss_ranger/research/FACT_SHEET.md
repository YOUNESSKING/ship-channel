# FACT SHEET: USS Ranger (CV-61), "The Top Gun Carrier Sold for One Cent" (working title)

Confidence: **HIGH** = two or more reliable independent sources agree, at least one official/primary ·
**MED** = one official source, or reliable sources that differ in detail · **UNVERIFIED** = fewer than two reliable
sources, or only press/secondary/estimate. **Nothing here has passed the 3-checker gate yet (CLAUDE.md).**
Narrative and reasoning: `RESEARCH.md`. Compiled 2026-11-19.

**READ FIRST: three corrections to the brief.**
1. **The one-cent contract was awarded 22 Dec 2014, not 2017.** The tow ran 5 Mar to 12 Jul 2015. Scrapping finished 1 Nov 2017. (The "2017" in the brief is the *end* of the story, and also the year the sister carrier Independence was towed.)
2. **"One cent" is not true of every earlier carrier.** Forrestal (2013) and Saratoga (2014) were one cent; **Constellation was $3 million** (NAVSEA, June 2014); Independence's price was not found. See section F.
3. **Ranger was not "the" Top Gun carrier.** The film's main carrier was USS Enterprise (CVN-65); Ranger was used for some scenes while docked at North Island. See section C and the wording advice there.
