# Mallows Bay: higher-quality video (HQ pass)

Built 2026-11-15. Files in `src/film/hq_*.mp4` (git-ignored, about 480 MB in total). Contact sheets show one frame per second with the timestamp burned in: `sheets/hq_<stem>_N.jpg`, 30 s per sheet. The machine-readable copy is in `footage.json` under "films", with stems starting `hq_` (group "HQ"). **Nothing here is committed.**

## Rules for using these files
- **NOAA Earth Is Blue videos.** The footage was shot by NOAA staff (credits below), so it is a US federal work and in the public domain. Their music tracks are licensed (Universal Production Music, Killer Tracks), so **always strip the audio**. Nearly every shot has burned-in caption boxes and a small "earth is blue" logo in the bottom-right. Use only the clean windows listed below, or crop or blur the text.
- **The wk240 film credits "Additional footage courtesy of Marine Robotics and Remote Sensing, Duke University".** That footage is NOT public domain. The aerial mosaic and the low rib flyover at 15-24 s look like Duke footage: do not use them. For the other drone shots (kayaks, the overgrown hull, the rib flyover at 67-69 s) the owner should confirm they are McIntosh/NOAA. The NOAA web photo of the same kayak scene is credited "Matt McIntosh/NOAA".
- **The Accomac** (rusted steel hull with portholes, ospreys and eagles nesting on it) is a 1928 car ferry, NOT one of the WWI wooden steamers. Never caption it as the ghost fleet itself.
- **Not used:** `wk266 Stories from the Blue: Ocean Guardian` was downloaded, but it was filmed on California beaches, not at Mallows Bay, so it was deleted. Also skipped: `20230526-force-blue.mp4` (207 MB, a volunteer group's seagrass survey, credit not checked), archive.org `Mallows_Bay_Shipwrecks` (Prince George's CTV, 480p, no licence) and the Charles County Government TV clips (no licence; Maryland county works are not public domain).

## Priority 2: WWI shipbuilding film
- `hq_wartime_shipbuilding_1080`: NARA serves 111-H-1166 as a **1920x1080 5 Mbps** file (`111-h-1166_5Mbps.mp4`). The existing `wartime_shipbuilding.mp4` was a 480p transcode of it. The timing is identical (521.46 s), so the old shot list carries over unchanged. The picture is 4:3, pillarboxed. Sharper, but still a dupe print. The wooden hull frames are at 114-117.9 s.
- `111-M-148` (Our Bridge of Ships) and FC-FC-119 (Hog Island): NARA only has these at 720x480. There is no higher-resolution file.
- No other NARA moving-image record that came up in searches for "wooden ship", "Emergency Fleet Corporation", "Shipping Board" or "shipyard 1918" shows 1917-19 wooden-ship building beyond what is already listed. LoC film search pages are blocked (Cloudflare).
