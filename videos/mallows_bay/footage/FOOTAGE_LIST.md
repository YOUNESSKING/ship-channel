# Mallows Bay ghost fleet - footage list

Built 2026-11-15 for the Mallows Bay video. Files sit in `src/` (git-ignored): `src/photos/` (stills, longest side capped at 3840 px, JPEG q90; LoC items from the master TIFFs on tile.loc.gov, NARA items from the catalog's full scans), `src/film/` (NARA reels transcoded to 480p H.264). Contact sheets (one frame every 2 s, timestamp burned in) are in `sheets/<stem>_NN.jpg`. Machine-readable copy: `footage.json`. **Nothing here is committed.**

**Rights basis.** Every LoC item's `rights_information` and every NARA item's `useRestriction` field was read through the official JSON (loc.gov `?fo=json`, catalog.archives.gov proxy API) and is quoted verbatim below. LoC National Photo Co., Harris & Ewing and Bain = "No known restrictions on publication". NARA 111-SC (Signal Corps), 18-AA (Air Corps), 4-P (Food Administration/EFC posters) = federal works. NARA 165-WW is the *American Unofficial Collection of WWI Photographs*: NARA marks each "Unrestricted", but many prints are by commercial agencies (Underwood & Underwood, Central News) or the yards themselves; all are pre-1931 and the card dates show 1917-19. Ford Motor Co. films (FC-FC) and 111-H-1166 carry NARA "Restricted - Possibly" (copyright note quoted); all were made/released before 1931, so they are US public domain by age, but the owner should confirm before using them. NOAA images: only photos credited "<name>/NOAA" (federal staff) were taken; Duke University/NOAA mosaics, MD DNR, Mariners' Museum etc. were skipped. USGS/NAIP orthoimagery is a US Government work. Nothing NC/ND.

**Honesty rule.** The "does NOT show" column is binding: never caption a shot with a place, date, ship or person it does not verifiably show. **No archive film of the Mallows Bay / Widewater burn was found**: NARA's own note on 111-H-1166 says the Mallows Bay burning footage listed in agency papers "is not present on any copies held by NARA". The burn is covered only by the five Signal Corps aerial photos and two 1925 newspaper halftones.

Counts: 108 photos/maps/pages, 10 films (100.5 min of film).
