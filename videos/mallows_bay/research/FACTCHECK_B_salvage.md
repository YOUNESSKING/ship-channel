## E. The removal plan and PEPCO (1963–1970)

The PEPCO angle is **real and on the record**, but most popular versions get it wrong. The 1970 hearing (HRG70) is the
primary source. What it shows: in 1963 a dummy company bought the farm next to the wrecks for the power company; in 1964
that company refused a cheap Army plan to pin the wrecks in place and pushed for federal removal; in 1968 Congress ordered
removal (if Maryland paid half); in July 1970 a PEPCO vice-president admitted under oath that the company was a front.

## Items too good to be true (summary)
1. **"PEPCO's secret nuclear plant"** (NRHP) and **"coal barges"** (IT99): wrong. It was an oil-fired plant at Sandy Point.
2. **"Clear violation of SEC rules"**: not supported; the FPC said such straw purchases were legal.
