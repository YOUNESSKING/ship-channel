## Conflicts summary (script must pick one wording)
1. **Distance:** 32 mi crow-flies (computed) / 43 mi road / NOAA "40 miles" / DNR & Shomette "30 miles".
2. **Wreck count:** 141 (NOAA web) / 142 (FEIS) / 147 (Duke drone) / 187 archival (NRHP) / "nearly 200" (DNR) / "more than 100" WWI (FR19).
3. **Ships in the bay:** 169 (company claim) vs 152/154 (1929 Army map).
4. **Ships built:** 322 incl. composites (NRHP/FEIS) vs 285 wooden by Aug 1920 (SHOM01) vs "nearly 300" (NOAA).
5. **Shipyards:** 40+ in 17 states (FR19/FEIS) vs 87 (SHOM01) vs 189 in 26 states (NOAAWR).
6. **Sale price:** $750,000 (SHOM01) vs $862,000 (NRHP); 233 vs 234 ships.
7. **Cost per ship:** >$750,000 (NRHP) vs $700,000–$1M (SHOM01).
8. **1925 burn location:** Widewater VA (Evening Star) vs Sandy Point/Mallows Bay MD (AP, NRHP).
9. **Fires:** FEIS conflates Oct 1922 Alexandria (*Alanthus*) and Apr 1923 Widewater fires.
10. **Accomac built:** 1928 (NRHP, NOAA) vs 1918 / "1973" (FEIS errors).
11. **Western Marine end:** "bankruptcy" (SHOM01, NOAA) vs abandonment then dissolution Dec 1932 (NRHP).
12. **Brothels/stills:** reported (SHOM01) vs "no evidence" (NRHP).
13. **Nomination area:** 18 sq mi (DNR) vs 14 sq mi (NOAA19).
14. **Hurricane Isabel year:** NRHP "2004" (actual 2003).
