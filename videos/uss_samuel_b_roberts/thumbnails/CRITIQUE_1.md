## Worst issues found, fixed
1. Ship cut-out looked like a paper collage (hand polygon on a low-contrast print: ragged mast, grey sky pockets). Replaced by a no-cut-out method: the print is multiplied into the canvas so the sky becomes the canvas colour (no seam), the hull keeps its own greys (lifted), mast drawn from the print. Ship pixels are the original photograph.
2. T1 depth-line arrow sat in the bottom-right corner, T3 words covered Yamato's bow, and the ships in T1/T4 were small. Arrow moved up (ends y 515); Yamato crop shifted right; T1 ship 880 to 920 px, T4 780 to 820 px.

## Remaining (not fixed)
- Faint speckle at the hull edge where sky shows through the superstructure (visible only at full size, invisible at sidebar size).
- The Roberts is shown afloat; it is the real 1944 photo, so the thumbnail must not be read as the wreck. No wreck image or Caladan Oceanic material is used anywhere.
- T3: Yamato is from NARA 80-G-48888 (Samar, 25 Oct 1944); the "they" claim is the title promise, confirm in the script that Roberts was in the destroyer-escort torpedo run at Kurita's force.
