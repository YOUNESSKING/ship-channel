# USS Samuel B. Roberts: v2 changes (owner notes 2026-11-20)

Owner: "sound effects too loud; the hook is weak, just photos, irrelevant photos; make it engaging, open on something engaging; upload the critiques."
Two independent critiques were run first: `CRITIQUE_SOUND_v1.md` (measured on the v1 mix stems) and `CRITIQUE_HOOK_v1.md` (frame-by-frame).

## Sound (CRITIQUE_SOUND_v1)
- Measured: hit / boom / impact / explosion cues sat +2 to +5 LU over the voice; the explosions were clamped 7-9 dB by the limiter (hard bang).
- Fix (per-cue cuts only, the channel's locked levels untouched): hit -3 dB, boom -2 dB, impact -3 dB, explosion -4.5 dB + 6 kHz lowpass
  (softer transient); pops stacked on a big hit dropped. Beds unchanged (already 14-24 LU under the voice).

## Hook (0:00-0:53, CRITIQUE_HOOK_v1)

  (was a 1920 px profile and an almost empty horizon).

  bars Roberts vs Titanic on "Titanic", Roberts depth vs Kilimanjaro height on "Kilimanjaro", then a bright "≈3,000 ft" card.

  battle photos under "the smallest warships" and "the biggest guns" (shell splashes), the St. Lo explosion under "not to expect to survive".
