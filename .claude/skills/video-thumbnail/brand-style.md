CHANNEL STYLE (prehistory channel, from a study of Fossil's top thumbnails, see research/THUMBNAIL_STYLE.md):
- No headshot. The hero is ONE huge creature, close and cropped by the frame, filling the left 55-65%. A human element (a hand, a face, a swimmer) is allowed and must be kept when the prompt asks for it.
- Anatomy must be correct for the named animal (e.g. an orca has a rounded head, cone-shaped teeth, a white eye patch; never shark teeth).
- Dark, gritty, realistic digital oil painting, like a horror book cover: heavy texture, brush strokes, film grain. Not a cartoon, not a photo, no comic googly eyes.
- One strong light source: firelight, a torch or a warm rim light against near-black shadow. Palette: deep black, burnt orange, ember red, also for ice and sea topics: never deep blue (owner, Oct 2026). The cold reads through frost, icicles, breath vapour, white ice lit amber.
- Eyes: small glowing white or pale points in shadowed eye sockets (creepy, cinematic).
- The right 35-40% stays dark and empty (text is added later in post). Nothing important in the bottom-right corner.
- Generate WITHOUT any text, letters or logos.
