## Title lessons
1. Be first. Clones of hit titles got almost nothing (578 vs 769K; 179 vs 550K).
2. Famous ship + a reason it's impossible. The same templates on obscure ships flop (1–5K).
3. For second-tier ships, put one concrete odd fact in the title ("sold for 1 cent", "planes still on deck").
